# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 3, 2026**

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address (required for registration)
- Display name and username
- Optional: profile picture, biography, skills, website URL, location

### Content You Post
- Project updates, comments, milestone names, and descriptions
- Direct messages (stored and transmitted like any other content)

### Usage Data
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- IP address
- Pages visited and interactions
- Session duration and referral sources

## How We Use Your Information

- Operate and maintain the Platform
- Provide customer support
- Send account-related notifications (password resets, etc.)
- Improve and develop new features
- Detect and prevent abuse

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data:

- With service providers who help operate the Platform (hosting, email delivery)
- To comply with legal obligations
- To enforce our Terms and Content Policy
- With your explicit consent

## Data Retention

- Account data is retained until you delete your account
- Content you post remains visible until deleted
- Deleted content may persist in backups for up to 30 days
- Session and activity logs are retained for 90 days

## Your Rights

- Access and export your data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Opt out of non-essential communications

## Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt
- JWT tokens with short expiration
- Regular security audits

## Children's Privacy

Forge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Accounts Other Jurisdictions

Forge is hosted in the United States. By using the Platform, you consent to your data being processed in the United States.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced via the Platform.
