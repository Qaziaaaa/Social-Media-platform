# Content Policy — Community Guidelines

**Last updated: July 3, 2026**

Forge is a platform for builders to share their work publicly. These guidelines help keep the community constructive and safe.

## What We Allow

- Project updates and progress reports
- Technical discussions and questions
- Constructive feedback and collaboration
- Links to your project, portfolio, or documentation
- Direct messages between builders

## What We Don't Allow

### Illegal Content
- Copyright-infringing material
- Fraudulent or deceptive content
- Content violating applicable laws

### Harassment & Abuse
- Targeted harassment or hate speech
- Threats of violence
- Bullying or intimidation
- Unwanted sexual advances or content

### Impersonation
- Pretending to be another person or organization
- Using misleading usernames or profiles

### Spam & Manipulation
- Unsolicited promotion or advertising
- Posting repetitive or irrelevant content
- Artificially inflating engagement
- Operating fake accounts or bots

### NSFW Content
- Pornography or sexually explicit material
- Graphic violence or gore
- Content depicting self-harm

## Enforcement

Violations are handled based on severity:

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Minor | Content removal + warning |
| Moderate | Temporary suspension (1-14 days) |
| Severe | Permanent account suspension |
| Legal | Report to appropriate authorities |

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, contact [appeals@forge.dev](mailto:appeals@forge.dev) with:

- Your username
- The action taken
- Why you believe it was incorrect

## Reporting

To report a violation, contact [abuse@forge.dev](mailto:abuse@forge.dev) or use the in-app reporting feature.

## Good Faith

These guidelines apply to all users equally. We reserve the right to take action on content or behavior that violates the spirit of the platform even if not explicitly listed.
